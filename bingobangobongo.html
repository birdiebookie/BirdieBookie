🎯 BINGO BANGO BONGO

Three points available on every hole.
BINGO = first player on the green.
BANGO = closest to the pin once everyone is on the green.
BONGO = first player to hole out.

Play order matters: the player farthest from the hole plays first.
POINT VALUE:
Each point = $1
